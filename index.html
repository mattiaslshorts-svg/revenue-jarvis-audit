Revenue Jarvis

Local, operator-controlled content workflow for two owned YouTube channels.

Human review before transport

Revenue Jarvis produces content locally, checks technical quality and rights evidence, and presents videos for Founder Review. Approved video hashes and permanent channel IDs are bound before transport. The owner remains in control of content and publication decisions.
